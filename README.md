# Maddie's Quest ❤️
Upload `index.html`, `style.css`, and `game.js` to GitHub and enable Pages in Settings → Pages. Controls: WASD/arrows move, E interact. Pool: click toward the target ball to aim, then E to shoot. Cai's sprite includes brown hair with red streak, green hoodie, headphones, bracelets, chin hair, light jeans and white shoes. Staff sprites differ. Coffee is an actual walk-to-and-collect task. Phase 10 has 10 cards (four 7s, four 3s, 5 and 9), no wilds. Birthday scene is from Cai's perspective and the journal collectible contains the supplied poem. Art is original pixel art inspired by cozy RPGs.
